Mangrove Fragmentation and Post-Hurricane Recovery, Puerto Rico
Ten years of Sentinel-2 imagery across two Puerto Rico mangrove sites after Hurricane Maria. Ceiba State Forest — contiguous, 500 m deep — took the stronger half of the storm and returned to pre-storm vegetation levels within three years. Guayanilla Bay — a fragmented strip under 200 m wide — took the weaker half and has degraded continuously for nine years. Spatial configuration predicted recovery better than storm intensity did.

Cassin's Sparrow Breeding Phenology, 1980–2024
Breeding-season length derived from ~109,000 species occurrence records across four decades. The national trend was weak and mildly negative; splitting by state reversed it, revealing that the aggregate had been averaging across regions moving in opposite directions. Texas declined sharply while New Mexico and Arizona increased.
